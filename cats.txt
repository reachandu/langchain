Cats are one of the most popular pets in the world.
They are known for their independence and playful behavior.
Most domestic cats sleep between 12 and 16 hours each day.
Cats use their whiskers to help sense nearby objects and spaces.
A group of kittens is called a kindle.
Cats communicate through meows, purrs, body language, and tail movements.
Many cats enjoy chasing toys that mimic the movement of small animals.
Domestic cats are descended from wildcats that lived thousands of years ago.
Regular veterinary checkups help keep cats healthy and active.
Cats make wonderful companions for millions of people around the world.
